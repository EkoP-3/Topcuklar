# voice.md — Topçuklar

Read by the yt-* skills (copy to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`).

## Who I am talking to

One person: a 13–30 year old who scrolls Shorts, knows Napoleon, Genghis Khan and Leonidas by name,
argues about "who would win" in the comments, and swipes away in two seconds if nothing moves.

## How I actually talk

There is no face and no host. A deep, young, epic narrator (ElevenLabs "Revenant") says only short
lines, in English:

- "Mehmed the Conqueror, who took Constantinople."
- "Versus!"
- "Harald Hardrada, the last great Viking."
- "Who wins? Comment your pick!"
- "Who should fight next? Tell me below!"

The fight itself is the content: a deterministic ball-battle simulation, each leader with a
cultural weapon, an ultimate, close calls and a finishing track on epic K.O.s.

## Words I never use

"Smash that like button", "insane", "you won't believe", "let's get into it", meme slang, emoji in
speech. The audience is serious about history.

## Words I do use

"Versus", "Who wins?", the leader's epithet ("the Conqueror", "the Lionheart", "the Impaler").

## What I will not claim

Invented historical facts or numbers. A leader fact must be sourced (sources go in the
description). The simulation result is a game, not a historical verdict.

## My format

Vertical 9:16, 25–40 seconds, no intro card, fight from the first second, "WHO WINS?" over the
fight, a K.O., a "WINS" card and "Who should fight next?". Serious tone, sparing sound effects.
